About Micro SaaS Kits

Micro SaaS Kits is a one-person shop run by Amos Mignery. We build small, self-contained developer products — starter kits, monitoring scripts, API tools, and productivity templates — that save you days of setup work on projects you'd rather be shipping.

What the products are

  • Starter kits & templates — boilerplate code, landing pages, decks, and SOP documents you download and adapt.
  • Monitoring & data tools — scripts and small apps that track prices, releases, deadlines, and public metrics.
  • Directories & reference lists — curated starting points for research and outreach.

How data tools work

Every monitoring or data tool we sell uses official public APIs and other publicly available data sources, respecting their terms of service. We do not sell databases of personal information, and our tools do not harvest private data about individuals.

Sales & support

All purchases are processed by Gumroad, which handles checkout, VAT, and secure delivery. If you have a question about a product, reach me via amosmignery.dev.
